Fool's Gold

Rules:
- The goal of the game is to be the player with the most gold when the game ends
- The game ends when the deck runs out of cards and at least one player doesn't have any more cards
- Each player's gold amount can only be a number between 0-9
- When players gain or lose gold, the gold amount wraps around the edges like pacman (goldAmount = goldAmount mod 10)
- When a player gets injured (in game), their next turn is skipped
- Each player can play a card from their hand or draw a card from the deck

Start:
- Each player starts with 5 cards
- Each player starts with 0 gold

Cards:
- Gold Card {gain gold: (1-4)}
- Booty Card {double player's gold}
- Doubloons Card {another player gains gold: (3-6)}
- Cannon Card {another player loses gold: (3-6)}
- Treasure Map Card {all players gain gold: (6-8)}
- Cracken Card {all players lose gold: (4-7), injures random player}
- Rum Card {gives all your gold to another player}
- Plank Card {another player gives all their gold to you}
- Cutlass Card {another player loses gold: (1-9), injures another player}
- Hook Card {injures another player}
- Plunder Card {steal another player's card}
- Wind Card {shuffles all player's cards and distributes evenly, remaining cards get discarded}






The goal of the game is to be the Player with the most points when the game ends.
Each player starts with 5 points. Points can increase and decrease as the game progresses, but cannot be negative.
The game ends when there are no cards remaining in any draw piles.

Players gain and lose points by playing cards. Different types of cards increase/decrease points differently and may affect other players.

There are 4 types of cards:
-- Point card: The Player who plays it gains points (6-10 points).
-- Attack card: Reduces points of another player (by 5-8 points). The Player who plays it also gains points (3-4 points).
-- Freeze card: Freezes another player for one turn. Also reduces the other Player's points (by 4-6 points) The Player who plays it also gains points (1-2 points).
-- Thief card: The Player who plays it steals a card from another Player and places it in their hand. Costs 1 point to play.

Players start with 3 random cards in hand. The remaining cards start in two decks:
-- the Mixed Deck that contains a mixture of all card types
-- the Damage Deck that contains a mixture of only cards that can do damage

Each turn, a Player can choose one of 3 options:
-- 1. Play a card from their hand
-- 2. Draw a random card from the Mixed Deck. They do not get to play it.
-- 3. Draw a random card from the Damage Deck. They must play it immediately, and they do not gain points from playing it, but other Players lose points.
